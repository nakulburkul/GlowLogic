Identify Your Skin Profile

🌿
Normal

Well-balanced skin. Not too oily, not too dry. Pores are generally fine, and skin is rarely sensitive.

💧
Dry

Produces less sebum than normal skin. Can feel tight, look flaky, or appear dull without adequate moisture.

✨
Oily

Produces excess sebum. Prone to enlarged, visible pores and frequent acne breakouts or blackheads.

🌗
Combination

Features both oily and dry areas. Typically oily in the T-zone (forehead, nose, chin) and normal or dry on the cheeks.

🌸
Sensitive

Easily irritated by new products, weather changes, or fragrances. Often reacts with redness, itching, or burning.

Common Conditions & Solutions

Hover over any active ingredient badge to see exactly how it scientifically treats the condition.

🔴

Acne & Breakouts

Caused by clogged pores, trapped bacteria, and excess sebum production inflaming the follicle.

Clinical Actives to Look For:
Salicylic Acid (BHA) Benzoyl Peroxide Niacinamide
🏜️

Severe Dryness / Eczema

A severely compromised or damaged skin barrier leading to trans-epidermal water loss, flaking, and itching.

Clinical Actives to Look For:
Ceramides Hyaluronic Acid Colloidal Oatmeal
🔥

Rosacea & Redness

A chronic inflammatory condition causing persistent redness, visible blood vessels, and heat sensitivity.

Clinical Actives to Look For:
Azelaic Acid Centella Asiatica Sulfur
🌑

Hyperpigmentation

Dark patches, sun spots, or post-acne marks caused by an overproduction of melanin in concentrated areas.

Clinical Actives to Look For:
Vitamin C Alpha Arbutin Glycolic Acid (AHA)
