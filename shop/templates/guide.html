Identify Your Skin Profile

🌿 Normal

Well-balanced skin. Not too oily, not too dry. Pores are generally fine, and skin is rarely sensitive.

💧 Dry

Produces less sebum than normal skin. Can feel tight, look flaky, or appear dull without adequate moisture.

✨ Oily

Produces excess sebum. Prone to enlarged, visible pores and frequent acne breakouts or blackheads.

🌗 Combination

Features both oily and dry areas. Typically oily in the T-zone (forehead, nose, chin) and normal or dry on the cheeks.

🌸 Sensitive

Easily irritated by new products, weather changes, or fragrances. Often reacts with redness, itching, or burning.

Common Conditions & Active Ingredients

Hover over any ingredient badge to see exactly how it treats the condition.

🔴 Acne & Breakouts

Caused by clogged pores, bacteria, and excess sebum production.

Key Ingredients: Salicylic Acid (BHA) Benzoyl Peroxide Niacinamide

🏜️ Eczema / Dryness

A damaged skin barrier leading to severe flaking, itching, and redness.

Key Ingredients: Ceramides Hyaluronic Acid Colloidal Oatmeal

🔥 Rosacea

Chronic condition causing redness, visible blood vessels, and sometimes bumps.

Key Ingredients: Azelaic Acid Centella Asiatica Sulfur

🌑 Hyperpigmentation

Dark patches or spots caused by excess melanin production.

Key Ingredients: Vitamin C Alpha Arbutin Glycolic Acid (AHA)
